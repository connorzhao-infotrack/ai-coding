🎯 How a success is counted — and why 5 is achievable. The order pool is small (<30 orders), but retries on the same order are fine — a one-off flaky attempt doesn't burn the order. An order counts toward the top-5 once InfoDialer has delivered both its figures: the water rate (Sydney Water) and the council rate. If that takes, say, 3 attempts on the Sydney Water leg and 2 on the council leg, it still counts as one success. So the milestone is well within reach — and in parallel we keep hardening the system so each order needs as few retries as possible.
