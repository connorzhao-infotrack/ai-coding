How to fill your timesheet

🔗 Quick links
  • 📝 Timesheet app — where you lodge your time
  • 🏖️ Leave history (Rippling → My Time → Requests) — look up the leave you've taken
  • 📅 NSW public holidays (nsw.gov.au) — official dates, don't log time on these
  • 📋 Timesheets - H2FY26 — the approved epics for the current period

🚨 Common mistakes (seen in past audits)

  • Time logged on public holidays or weekends.
  • More than 7.5 hours logged in a single day.
  • Time logged against tickets with no epic — finance re-assigns these to Tech Debt, blowing your 15% cap.
  • No time (or too little) logged against Research & Admin / Tech Debt when that's genuinely where the time went — log it honestly, just keep it under the cap.
  • Exceeding the total expected hours for the period (working days × 7.5, public holidays excluded).
⚡ TL;DR — Log your time in the timesheet app, only against the epics listed on the current period's Timesheets page (e.g. Timesheets - H2FY26). 7h 30m per working day, leave goes on the Leave epic, nothing on weekends or public holidays, and keep Research & Admin + Tech Debt under 15% of your total time.

🧭 Where to log time

  1. Open the timesheet app.
  2. Select the Timesheet tab in the top section.
  3. Select the date range you want to log time for.
  4. Click Import issues and add the epics for the current period (see next section).
  5. Fill in your hours against each epic, per day.

🎯 What to log time against

Log time directly against the epics — never against individual tickets. The approved epics for each period are listed on that period's Timesheets Confluence page (a new page is created each period — a half, a 4-month block, etc.).

For the current period (Timesheets - H2FY26) that means:

🔧 Utility SETTLEIT-2089 — Research & Admin 🔬 Research, meetings, admin, anything not tied to a Top 5 epic

♻️ BAU Top 5 SETTLEIT-9804, SETTLEIT-9809

📏 The rules

  • ⏱️ 7h 30m per working day. Never log more than 7.5 hours in a day.
  • 🚫 No time on weekends or public holidays. Leave those days empty (check the official NSW public holiday dates).
  • 🏖️ Leave goes on the Leave epic (SETTLEIT-6024), at 7h 30m per day of leave. You can look up the leave you've taken in Rippling → My Time → Requests.
  • 📋 Only use the epics on the current period's Timesheets page. Time logged on tickets without an approved epic gets re-assigned by finance (usually to Tech Debt), which can push you over the 15% cap.

⚖️ The 15% cap on Research & Admin + Tech Debt

Official rule: your combined time on Research & Admin (SETTLEIT-2089) + Tech Debt (SETTLEIT-573) must not exceed 15% of your total hours for the period.
Recommended: check the 15% on a monthly basis as you go, so you don't discover at the end of the period that you're way over.

🧮 Example (monthly check): a month with 21 working days = 21 × 7.5 = 157.5 hours. 15% of that is ≈ 23.5 hours — roughly 3 days. If your R&A + Tech Debt for the month is above that, rebalance toward your Top 5 epics.

✅ Checklist before the period closes

  • ☐ Every working day has exactly 7h 30m logged.
  • ☐ Weekends and public holidays are empty.
  • ☐ All leave is on SETTLEIT-6024.
  • ☐ Only epics from the current period's Timesheets page are used.
  • ☐ R&A + Tech Debt ≤ 15% of total hours.
